About Me

Engineering instincts.
Founder hunger. PM craft.

I am a Senior Product Manager with engineering roots and founder experience — a combination that makes me dangerous in the best possible way. I don't just manage products; I define categories, ship them into production in the highest-stakes regulated environments, and make them commercially successful.
